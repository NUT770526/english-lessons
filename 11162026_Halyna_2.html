Exercise 2. Offers or Requests?

Business English Practice: Can I / Shall I / Could you / Would you

Choose the correct option

Select the most suitable expression for each situation below.

Remember:
  • Offers — ви пропонуєте свою допомогу.
  • Requests — ви просите когось щось зробити.
1. You see your colleague carrying heavy boxes.
___ help you with those boxes?
2. You need your colleague to send you a document politely.
___ send me the document, please?
3. You want to offer to call a client.
___ call the client for you?
4. You politely ask a colleague to wait.
___ wait a moment, please?
5. Your colleague looks confused. You offer assistance.
___ explain the rules to you?
6. You need someone to check the prices.
___ check the prices for me, please?
7. You offer to send a report.
___ send you the report after the meeting?
8. You ask a colleague to speak politely to customers.
___ be more polite to our customers, please?
