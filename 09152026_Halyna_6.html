Exercise 2. Personal Questions & Practice

Ответьте на вопросы о себе. Нажмите "Show Sample Answers", чтобы увидеть примеры ответов.
Part A: Theatre
A1. How many times have you been to the theatre this month?
Sample answer: I have been to the theatre once this month.
A2. When did you go? What did you see? How did you like it?
Sample answer: I went last Saturday. I saw a drama performance and I liked it very much.
A3. Did you have good seats?
Sample answer: Yes, I had great seats in the stalls.
A4. Who did you go with?
Sample answer: I went there with my friends.
Part B: Weekend Trips
B1. How often do you go to the country for the weekend?
Sample answer: I go to the country almost every weekend.
B2. How many times have you been there this month?
Sample answer: I have been there twice this month.
B3. How long did you stay there? Did you have a good time?
Sample answer: I stayed there for two days, and I had a wonderful time.
Part C: Films & Cinema
C1. Have you seen any interesting films this week?
Sample answer: Yes, I have seen a very interesting documentary this week.
C2. When did you last see a good film?
Sample answer: I last saw a good film three days ago.
C3. Did you go to the cinema or did you watch it on the telly?
Sample answer: I watched it on the telly at home.
C4. What was the film about?
Sample answer: The film was about space exploration and science.
Part D: Reading Books
D1. How many good books have you read this year?
Sample answer: I have read about five good books this year.
D2. Which of them do you remember best?
Sample answer: I remember a detective novel best of all.
D3. Which of them did you like best?
Sample answer: I liked the historical novel best.
D4. Have you got enough time to read much?
Sample answer: Unfortunately, I don't have enough time to read as much as I want.
Part E: Newspapers & News
E1. Have you read a paper today?
Sample answer: No, I haven't read a paper today, but I read news online.
E2. Do you read papers every day?
Sample answer: I usually read online news every day.
E3. Is there any important news in today’s paper?
Sample answer: Yes, there are a few important articles about global economics.
E4. Do you usually read papers before you go to work (college) or when you get home?
Sample answer: I usually read the news when I get home in the evening.
Part F: Lectures
F1. How many lectures have there been today?
Sample answer: There have been three lectures today.
F2. What were they about?
Sample answer: They were about modern history and financial management.
F3. How many of them were interesting?
Sample answer: Two of them were very interesting.
