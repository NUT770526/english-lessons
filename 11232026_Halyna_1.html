Every successful businessman knows and practices good business etiquette. What this means is that they know the proper rules for meeting and interacting with business partners, co-workers, and new connections to build strong and valuable relationships. A businessman must be polite, respect the other person, listen to him or her, and give a firm handshake.

In a business meeting, it could be considered rude, or bad manners, to give advice when no one asks for it. So, a smart businessman should only give advice when it is requested. Knowing what to say and when to say it can help develop and promote a healthy and trusting working environment for everyone in the office or business.

Knowing when to give helpful advice and feedback can also lead to building better business connections and networks. This, in the future, can lead to increased business as the businessman receives more offers and requests for his goods and services.

Another part of business etiquette that is important is to be aware of partners' and co-workers' culture. If the businessman knows the traditions, customs, and what is acceptable to say to others of a different culture, then he can build trust, respect, and friendship with them. This will bring not only benefits to his company, but will create relationships that will last for many years.
