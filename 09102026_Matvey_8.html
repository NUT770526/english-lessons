Adolescence is a time of change and learning. Teens and young people are dealing with profound questions every day. They are learning who they are, what they want in the future, what interests them, what university they want to enroll at (if they want to), what society wants from them, and what life is all about. These questions, and even more, can be very confusing and difficult to solve. These questions also involve emotions. Teens are learning to understand their emotions and understand who they are. Sometimes if we don't know why we are having such emotions, or how to deal with them, we lose confidence in ourselves. This can be very dangerous because a teenager can accomplish any goal they want with great results, but without confidence then they may not even try. In this situation, teens need help and guidance. Parents can help their children and teen by helping them build self-esteem and confidence.

Dealing with all of this is no easy task, but it is normal. It takes a long time to mature, and even many adults are learning, growing and developing their maturity and emotions every day. All people, from children to teens to adults, need support and help from those around them to learn, grow and become better people. Life questions can be even harder for teenagers living in more rural, poorer, and vulnerable communities. University might never be an option for them because their families don't have money. Their biggest worry is food, clothing, shelter and the welfare of themselves and their family. The economic disparity makes the questions of life even more challenging because it is about survival. Regardless of the country or economic level of the family, and no matter where a teen lives, he is sure to experience intense and profound physical, emotional and social changes as he matures.
