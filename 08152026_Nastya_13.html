7. Complete the mini-interview
Доповни відповіді правдивою інформацією про себе.
💡 Як давати короткі відповіді:
Have you got...?
→ Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
Has he/she got...?
→ Yes, he/she has. / No, he/she hasn't.
Do you/they have to...?
→ Yes, I/they do. / No, I/they don't.
Does he/she have to...?
→ Yes, he/she does. / No, he/she doesn't.
