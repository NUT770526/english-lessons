Exercise 1. Present Perfect

Розкрийте дужки, використовуючи Present Perfect. Оберіть правильну форму з випадаючого списку. Натисніть 🔊, щоб прослухати речення.

1. I the client’s email. (read)
2. She the monthly report. (write)
3. We just the invoice. (send)
4. The finance director already the budget. (approve)
5. Our managers to the conference. (go)
6. you ever this sales report? (see)
7. The new accountant just to the office. (come)
8. Our team all the calculations. (do)
9. The company a final decision. (make)
10. The client to my email yet. (not reply)
