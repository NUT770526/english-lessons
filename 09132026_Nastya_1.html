Exercise 2. Rewrite the sentences

Перебудуй речення, використовуючи слово в дужках. Не змінюй значення.
1. Although she was frightened, she kept calm.
(DESPITE)
Correct: Despite being frightened, she kept calm.
2. Despite the huge earthquake, Harold survived unharmed.
(ALTHOUGH)
Correct: Although there was a huge earthquake, Harold survived unharmed.
3. In spite of her fear, Mum lifted the bookcase.
(ALTHOUGH)
Correct: Although she was afraid, Mum lifted the bookcase.
4. Mum put Harold under the table because she wanted to protect him.
(SO AS TO)
Correct: Mum put Harold under the table so as to protect him.
5. She moved carefully because she didn’t want to get injured.
(SO AS NOT TO)
Correct: She moved carefully so as not to get injured.
6. The earthquake was very frightening. Harold never forgot it.
(SO ... THAT)
Correct: The earthquake was so frightening that Harold never forgot it.
7. It was a very frightening experience.
(SUCH)
Correct: It was such a frightening experience.
8. The woman was very brave.
(SO)
Correct: The woman was so brave.
9. She was a very brave woman.
(SUCH)
Correct: She was such a brave woman.
10. The furniture was very heavy. She could hardly lift it.
(SO ... THAT)
Correct: The furniture was so heavy that she could hardly lift it.
