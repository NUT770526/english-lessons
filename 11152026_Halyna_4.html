1. Listen & Read (Transcript)

Statistics all across the world say that people who use the internet are becoming much more suspicious of surveillance by internet technology (IT) companies and the government. Some internet users believe that IT companies and the government are recording their video calls. Others even believe that IT companies can read the emails that we exchange with our colleagues, friends, and family.

A tiny amount of people think that the smartphones we buy have surveillance equipment, that the government uses to spy on us for national security reasons, inside of them. People who believe this may be a little paranoid.

If you go to your search engine and research this topic, you can find many websites that talk about this topic. What you will learn is that most of the information collected through websites and social media is used for advertising. Special settings in your browser, called cookies, allow websites to collect data about you and help advertising companies sell you products they think you may like. Most websites ask what cookies you want to accept. You can protect your privacy by not accepting all the cookies, but if you don't accept any cookies, the website may not work.

So, the next time you go to a website and browse smartphones, sports equipment, or something else you like, don't be surprised if you see a pop-up, or a banner, at the top of your screen that says "Buy it now! Great smartphones at a discount!"
