Exercise 2. Make the sentences negative

Перепишите предложения в отрицательной форме (**don't** / **doesn't**). Заглавные буквы и точки в конце предложений не учитываются при проверке.

1. I commute to the office every day.
2. Sarah gets to work by car.
3. We meet clients on Fridays.
4. The meeting starts at nine.
5. He works in the finance department.
6. They work with international customers.
7. My manager prefers public transport.
8. I get stuck in traffic every morning.
9. Our schedule changes every week.
10. She finds out about changes during meetings.
