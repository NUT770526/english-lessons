Sentence Transformation

5 Transformations

Combine each pair of negative ideas into one single sentence using the prompt in brackets. Remember to use an affirmative verb!

1.

Mark didn't oversleep. Sarah didn't oversleep either. (Use: Neither ... nor ...)

2.

The first hotel didn't have a record of my reservation. The second hotel didn't have one either. (Use: Neither of ...)

3.

He couldn't persuade the guide. He couldn't persuade the driver either. (Use: He could persuade neither ... nor ...)

4.

This jacket isn't suitable for slush. These shoes aren't suitable for slush either. (Use: Neither ... nor ...)

5.

Anna didn't leap into her clothes quickly. Kate didn't leap into her clothes quickly either. (Use: Neither of ...)
