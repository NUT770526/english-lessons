Exercise 2. Which one would you choose? 🎯

Choose A or B and explain your answer. Use the word in bold in your answer.

1. turn down
[tɜːn daʊn] відхилити, відмовити
Your friend offers you a ticket to a concert, but you have an important test tomorrow.
A. Go to the concert.
B. Turn it down and study.
2. accountable teammate
[əˈkaʊntəbl ˈtiːmmeɪt] відповідальний тиммейт
Who is a better teammate?
A. Someone who always does their part on time.
B. Someone who often forgets about team tasks.
3. manage time
[ˈmænɪdʒ taɪm] управляти часом
You have homework, football practice and a new game you want to play. What do you do?
4. underdog
[ˈʌndədɒɡ] аутсайдер, слабша сторона
Your favourite team is playing against the strongest team in the league. Everyone thinks your team will lose.
5. knock on wood
[nɒk ɒn wʊd] постукати по дереву (щоб не приврочити)
You haven't been late for school this month.
6. camaraderie
[ˌkæməˈrɑːdəri] товариськість, дух товариства
What creates better camaraderie in a team?
A. Helping each other.
B. Blaming each other for mistakes.
