4. Read these three teenagers' ideas about family and friends.

Do you agree or disagree? Why?
a) "My family is very important to me. I love my grandparents, my parents, my brothers and sisters, and my cousins. My family are everything to me."
Key Vocabulary:
important /ɪmˈpɔː.tənt/ важный
grandparents /ˈɡræn.peə.rənts/ бабушка и дедушка
cousins /ˈkʌz.ənz/ двоюродные братья/сестры
everything /ˈev.ri.θɪŋ/ всё
b) "I talk to my friends about my problems, not to my family. My friends help me. They know me and understand me very well."
Key Vocabulary:
problems /ˈprɒb.ləmz/ проблемы
understand /ˌʌn.dəˈstænd/ понимать
c) "I think it's important to talk to your family and to have good friends. My family always help me and my friends are always fun. I like to be with my family and with my friends."
Key Vocabulary:
always /ˈɔːl.weɪz/ всегда
fun /fʌn/ веселые / весело
