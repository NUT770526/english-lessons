Exercise 1. Визнач час

Прочитай та прослухай речення. Обери правильний час для кожного речення:
PS — Present Simple, PaS — Past Simple або PP — Present Perfect.

1. Kate is always cheerful and friendly.
2. My older brother was very bossy yesterday.
3. Have you ever admired a famous author?
4. Lucy has just won an award.
5. Ben worked part-time last summer.
6. I usually get on well with my sibling.
7. Has your mate given you any advice yet?
8. They did not keep quiet during yesterday's lesson.
9. Tom often makes us laugh.
10. We have recently spent a lot of time together.
