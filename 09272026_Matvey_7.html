Exercise 2. Put the word in the correct place
Перепиши предложение и поставь слово в правильное место.
Example:
I have finished my essay. (already)
→ I have already finished my essay.
1. The explorer has visited the rainforest. (recently)
2. She has arrived on the island. (just)
3. Have you finished your essay? (yet)
4. We have seen this film about volcanoes. (already)
5. He hasn't visited the desert. (yet)
6. They have returned from their journey. (just)
7. I have learned about this famous explorer. (recently)
8. Has she finished her difficult project? (yet)
