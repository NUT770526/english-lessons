1c. Family & Friends Story 📖

Последовательное интерактивное упражнение (без прошедшего времени)
Шаг 1. Complete the table
Member / Friend Nationality City Job Character
Шаг 2. Answer the questions (Written & Oral Practice)
1. Where is your family from?
2. What nationality are your parents?
3. Where are your grandparents from?
4. What are your parents' jobs?
5. What are your grandparents' jobs?
6. What are the members of your family like?
Шаг 3. Tell about your family & Add your own sentences
Пример рассказа (Present Simple):
My family is from Ukraine. My mother is an accountant. She is kind and friendly. My father is a manager. He is clever and generous. My grandmother is a teacher from Izmail, and my grandfather is an engineer from Odesa. I love my family very much.
